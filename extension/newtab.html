To-do list

Keep your tasks here, or show a list from an app you already use. Ticking, editing and adding tasks updates that app too.

Where do I find it?

In Todoist, open Settings › Integrations › Developer and copy your API token. It stays on this computer.

Calendar

Where do I find the link?

Google Calendar: on calendar.google.com open Settings, pick your calendar, and copy Secret address in iCal format.

iCloud: in the Calendar app, right-click the calendar, choose Share Calendar, tick Public Calendar and copy the link. Anyone with an iCloud public link can see that calendar.

Add more than one calendar by putting each link on its own line. Links stay on this computer.

The widget checks for changes every 15 minutes, so a new event can take a few minutes to appear.

Chrome and Google

Works on google.com in light and dark mode. Chrome will ask to let Glass Tab change google.com. Reload Google after turning it on.

Top bar

Blends the page into Chrome's top bar. Pick Glass Tab theme only if you installed one of the Glass Tab toolbar themes.

Chrome's footer

Chrome adds a bar along the bottom of extension new tabs. To hide it, right-click that bar and choose Hide footer on New Tab page.

Backup

Save your settings to a file, or load them from one, for example on another computer. To-do sign-ins, calendar links and your photo aren't in the file.
