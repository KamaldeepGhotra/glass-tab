Calendar

Where do I find the link?

Google Calendar: on calendar.google.com open Settings, pick your calendar, and copy Secret address in iCal format.

iCloud: in the Calendar app, right-click the calendar, choose Share Calendar, tick Public Calendar and copy the link. Anyone with an iCloud public link can see that calendar.

Add more than one calendar by putting each link on its own line. Links stay on this computer.

The widget checks for changes every 15 minutes, so a new event can take a few minutes to appear.
